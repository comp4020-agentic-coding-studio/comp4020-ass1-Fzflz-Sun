About this model

This is a simplified teaching model, not driving instruction. One car, one broad fixed corner, symmetric front/rear load, and a linear tyre model with a friction-circle limit per axle. Steering, throttle, and braking all draw on the same per-axle grip budget; drivetrain only changes which axle carries the longitudinal share — FWD does not always understeer, RWD does not always oversteer, and AWD can still slide if pushed hard enough. Dry/wet/ice are a relative, illustrative grip ordering, not measured coefficients for any real tyre or surface. Full assumptions and tuned constants are documented in this project's docs/model-assumptions.md.
